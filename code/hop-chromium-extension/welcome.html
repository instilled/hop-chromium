Welcome to Hop Sidebar

Arc's sidebar, in your browser. A few steps and you're set.

1 Open the sidebar

Hop Sidebar is in Vivaldi's panel bar on the left: click its icon (a ring with a dot). To open and close it from the keyboard, use ⌘E → Toggle Current Panel, or bind that command in Settings → Keyboard.

Keep the panel non-floating (⌘E → Toggle Floating Panel), or it closes whenever you click the page.

Click Hop Sidebar's toolbar icon, or press ⌃S. Pin the icon from the puzzle-piece menu so it stays in the toolbar.

For an Arc-like layout, put the panel on the left: Settings → Appearance → Side panel → Show on left.

2 Pin your sites

Drag a tab from the sidebar's tab list, or a link from any page, onto the top of the sidebar. Pinned sites show as tiles, like Arc's favorites, and ⌘1–⌘6 open the first six. Hover the Gmail, Google Calendar or Google Chat tile for a preview.

Pinned sites live in a bookmarks folder named Pinned; the rest of your bookmarks bar shows below them. Each bookmark keeps its own tab, like Arc's pinned tabs.

3 Coming from Arc? Bring your sidebar

Arc keeps its sidebar in a file. Choose it here and Hop turns your favorites into pinned sites, and each space's pinned tabs into a bookmark folder (with Arc's folders inside).

  1. Click Choose Arc's sidebar file, then press ⌘⇧G in the file dialog.
  2. Paste the path and press Return: ~/Library/Application Support/Arc/StorableSidebar.json

The file is read here in your browser and goes nowhere else.

4 Shortcuts

⌃S shows and hides the sidebar, and ⌘1–⌘3 open the first pinned sites. Browsers only allow four preset shortcuts, so set ⌘4–⌘6 yourself.

If ⌘1–⌘6 still switch tabs, Vivaldi's own Switch to Tab 1–6 shortcuts win: clear them in Settings → Keyboard.

5 Calendar preview (optional)

To see today's and tomorrow's meetings when you hover the Calendar tile, paste your calendar's secret iCal address.

Stored only in this browser profile. Gmail and Chat previews need no setup.

6 Slim it down (optional)

A small Vivaldi UI mod hides the panel bar, lets Hop take its place at 220 px, and removes the panel's title row and border, for a single sidebar like Arc's.


  1. It goes to a folder Hop Sidebar Vivaldi mod in your Downloads.
  2. Open vivaldi://experiments , tick Allow for using CSS modifications, and restart Vivaldi.
  3. Settings → Appearance → Custom UI Modifications → Select Folder → choose Downloads/Hop Sidebar Vivaldi mod. Restart Vivaldi again.

If the sidebar looks cut off on the right afterwards, click into it once: Vivaldi keeps the panel's old width until then. To undo, clear the folder in Custom UI Modifications.
