3 Coming from Arc? Bring your sidebar

Arc keeps its sidebar in a file. Choose it here and Hop turns your favorites into pinned sites, and each space's pinned tabs into a bookmark folder (with Arc's folders inside).

  1. Click Choose Arc's sidebar file, then press ⌘⇧G in the file dialog.
  2. Paste the path and press Return: ~/Library/Application Support/Arc/StorableSidebar.json

Favorites → pinned sites

Spaces → a bookmark folder each

The file is read here in your browser and goes nowhere else.
